
# NEXGEN Weather Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 27, 2026

NEXGEN Weather is a browser extension by NEXGEN Dev Studio. This policy
describes what the extension does with information when you use it.

## Information the extension handles

### Saved locations and settings

The extension stores your selected weather locations, favorites, temperature
unit, theme, refresh interval, and notification preference in the browser's
local extension storage.

This information is not sent to NEXGEN Dev Studio.

### Weather and location requests

When you search for a city or load weather for a saved location, the extension
sends the city search text or the selected location's latitude and longitude
to Open-Meteo's public geocoding, forecast, and air-quality services.

NEXGEN Weather does not operate a NEXGEN server that receives or stores these
requests.

The extension does not use the browser's physical geolocation API in this
release. Location access is based on locations selected by the user.

### Notifications

If you enable weather notifications, the extension periodically checks the
selected location's weather and may display a browser/operating-system
notification. Notification preference and schedule are stored locally.

## What NEXGEN Weather does not do

NEXGEN Weather does not intentionally:

- create a NEXGEN account for you;
- collect your name, email address, passwords, or browsing history;
- sell personal information;
- display advertising or tracking scripts;
- send saved settings or location lists to NEXGEN Dev Studio.

## Third-party services

Weather data and geocoding are supplied by Open-Meteo. Requests to those
services are subject to the provider's own privacy policy, terms, and
technical practices.

## Data retention

Local extension data remains in your browser until you remove it, clear the
extension's storage, uninstall the extension, or otherwise modify browser
storage.

NEXGEN Dev Studio does not maintain a separate copy of that local extension
data.

## Children's privacy

NEXGEN Weather is not designed to collect personal information from children.
Because the extension does not require an account or intentionally collect
personal information, it does not knowingly maintain a personal profile of
child users.

## Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the extension's data practices change.
The effective date above will be updated for material revisions.
